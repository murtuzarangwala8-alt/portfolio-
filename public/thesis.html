What We Are Truly Doing: Quantitative Methodology

Transparency is the bedrock of quantitative empirical asset pricing. Here is how my machine learning system processes data, prevents data leakage, models microstructure frictions, and executes live trades.

01

Point-in-Time Data & 59 Features

Features update strictly on Bloomberg publication timestamps (earnings_announcement_date) rather than fiscal quarter end-dates, eliminating lookahead bias. Fundamentals expire after 90 trading days to prevent stale financial ratios.

• 16 Technical • 30 Fundamental • 2 Sentiment • 5 Macro • 6 Betas
02

TFDMGA Deep Sequence Network

Combines Causal 1D TCN encoders (R=15d receptive field) with Sequential Ring Attention (Tech ← Sent ← Fund ← Macro) and 3-Way Macro Dynamic Gating (τ=0.50) to dynamically reweight feature modalities across market regimes.

• Out-of-sample IC: +0.0348 • ICIR: 3.12 • DM Stat: 2.41 (p=0.016 vs LSTM)
03

Microstructure Frictions & 2:1 TPSL

Signals generated at close of day $t$ execute at open of day $t+1$ (1-day delay). Every trade deducts 10 bps round-trip friction and turnover rebalancing, paired with a 2:1 Take-Profit/Stop-Loss (+4% TP / -2% SL) circuit breaker.

• $1,000 USD deposit compounds to $6,482.10 net of 10 bps fees
04

Fama-French Spanning & EMH Proof

Fama-French 5-factor regressions yield a net strategy alpha of α = -0.18% per year (p = 0.976). Because net alpha is zero, 100% of strategy returns represent dynamic factor risk compensation (RMW β = +0.512, t = +9.12), matching Fama's Efficient Market Hypothesis.
